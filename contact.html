Common questions

Frequently asked.

How quickly can you arrange a flight?

For standard bookings, same-day arrangements are common. For VIP protocol or charters we recommend 24 to 48 hours notice, though we handle urgent requests at any hour.

Do you handle both local and international flights?

Yes. We procure tickets and manage logistics for domestic Nigerian routes and international destinations across all major airlines.

How is my travel information kept confidential?

Client data is treated with the highest level of discretion. We operate strict internal privacy protocols and do not share itinerary or personal details with anyone outside the necessary travel providers.

Can you set up a travel office for my company?

Absolutely. We set up comprehensive Logistics, Protocol and Travelling Offices for corporate clients and can manage the whole operation on an outsourcing basis.

Do you provide security escorts for high-profile individuals?

Yes. We offer personalized armed security escorts who safely transfer passengers between airports, residences and offices, with full discretion.
